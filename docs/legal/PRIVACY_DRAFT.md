Deckastra processes account identity (Google basic profile/email or verified email-link identity), cloud workspace membership, saved cloud decks and their assets, usage/credit records, and operational diagnostics. Desktop decks are stored locally unless you explicitly use a cloud feature. Local editing and deterministic export do not require an AI account. Signing out does not delete local files.

When you request AI assistance, the selected text, images and instructions needed for that request are sent to our Google Cloud gateway and Vertex AI/Gemini. Cloud Translation and Text-to-Speech process the content explicitly submitted to those features. Local-desktop inference requests are not persisted as full deck records in the gateway. Saved cloud decks and committed proposals persist as workspace content; temporary assistant payloads expire after completion or abandonment. Operational spans exclude prompt/deck text. We do not authorize Google to train or fine-tune models using your customer data.

Google is our cloud processor for hosting, PostgreSQL, private object storage, identity and AI. Google's training restriction requires permission before customer data is used for training. Its retention documentation describes exceptions such as abuse monitoring and optional grounding features; we do not promise blanket zero retention. Our database and object storage are in `asia-south1`; AI configured on `global` may be processed outside India. The exact model and enabled features must be reflected here before AI launch. See [Google Cloud service terms](https://cloud.google.com/terms/service-terms), [data-processing addendum](https://cloud.google.com/terms/data-processing-addendum), and [AI retention documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/vertex-ai-zero-data-retention), checked 2026-10-05.

Deleting a cloud account revokes Deckastra bearer access immediately and queues removal of active cloud account data after a 16-minute signed-link/job grace period. Transfer sole ownership of shared workspaces first. Private export objects expire after 30 days; PostgreSQL backups and GCS soft-deleted data can remain for their seven-day retention windows. Minimal deletion receipts and required abuse/usage records are subject to the implemented maintenance periods. Local files and copies already shared with others remain under their owners' control. **[Confirm any statutory retention, support-log retention and rights/complaints process before publication.]**

We use encryption in transit and managed encryption at rest, distinct service identities, private buckets and bounded signed links. Electron credentials use operating-system protected storage and are not exposed to the renderer.
